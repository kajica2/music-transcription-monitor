🔥 Hot Take

MuScriptor (arXiv:2607.08168) is the most impactful paper of the week, and arguably of the year for the AMT field. Three reasons:

1. It's the first open-weight multi-instrument AMT model that actually works on real-world music. Previous "open" models either needed isolated stems (Basic Pitch, MT3 in single-instrument mode) or collapsed on genre transfer. MuScriptor's 170 k real-song training set + RL post-training closes a gap that has been open since Gardner et al. 2022. 2. It applies the LLM-era playbook — large-scale pretraining → real-data fine-tuning → RL alignment — to AMT specifically. That three-stage recipe is the same one that made ChatGPT / Suno / HeartMuLa work, and applying it to AMT is the clearest sign that the symbolic-music subfield is going to converge with the generative-audio subfield in 2026–2027. 3. It's reproducible today. pip install muscriptor, point it at a WAV, get a MIDI. For a swarm-tracking project, this is the first entry in months where the GitHub repo is the more important artifact than the paper. Honorable mention: VocalParse for showing the LALM path (Qwen3-ASR backbone + interleaved CoT) is competitive on singing transcription and ships with code. And MuseAgent-1 is the architecture-pattern paper to keep referencing when we design our own AMT-aware agent. The "AMT-as-token-prediction" paradigm won 2025/2026. The "AMT-as-LLM-tool-use" paradigm (MuseAgent, Libretto, Text2Score) is the one to watch for 2027. ---

Paper #1 · Rouard et al., 2026

MuScriptor — Open Multi-Instrument AMT Foundation Model

arXiv:2607.08168 · Submitted 9 Jul 2026

arXiv: arXiv:2607.08168

Summary: MuScriptor recasts multi-instrument AMT as autoregressive MIDI-token generation (MT3-style) over a decoder-only Transformer that reads a mel-spectrogram. The kicker is the third training stage: an RL pass over high-quality real data, plus optional conditioning on a known instrument set at inference time — that's the knob that lets one model transcribe "anything from classical to heavy metal." Reported multi-instrument F1 is 48.2 vs. 21.9 for YourMT3+ on the same eval.

Reproducibility: ✅ **Open weights + code released.** Code on GitHub: (MIT inference code, CC BY-NC 4.0 weights). Three sizes (small ≈100 M, medium ≈300 M, large ≈1.3 B) on Hugging Face under the `MuScriptor/` org. `pip install muscriptor` works; a `uvx muscriptor serve` web UI is also documented.

Paper #2 · Zhao et al., 2026

MuseAgent-1 — Multimodal Agent for Music Scores + Performance Audio

arXiv:2601.11968 · Submitted 17 Jan 2026

arXiv: arXiv:2601.11968

Summary: MuseAgent is an *architecture-pattern* paper: stick a CQT-based AMT module (audio→MusicXML/JSON) and a measure-wise OMR module (sheet image→ABC) in front of an LLM, plus a retrieval layer over symbolic + audio libraries, and you get a system that can actually answer "what key is this passage modulating to?" — something stock MLLMs like Gemini-2.5-Pro routinely fail at. With GPT-4.1 as the orchestrator, MuseAgent hits 79.1% accuracy on the audio slice of MuseBench.

Reproducibility: ⚠️ **Partial.** The paper is a tech report and the underlying ICLR 2026 submission is at . No first-party public GitHub repo linked from the abstract; the AMT and OMR modules themselves rely on existing baselines. Worth pinging the authors for a code drop.

Paper #3 · Chen et al., 2026

VocalParse — Singing-Voice Transcription with a Large Audio Language Model

arXiv:2605.04613 · Submitted 6 May 2026

arXiv: arXiv:2605.04613

Summary: VocalParse fine-tunes Qwen3-ASR-1.7B (a Whisper-encoder + Qwen-LLM-decoder LALM) to emit *interleaved* lyric + pitch + note-value + global-BPM tokens in one autoregressive pass. The CoT trick — generate plain lyrics first, then the structured score — is what makes the model robust on OOD singing. They back it with **SingCrawl**, a web-scale pseudo-labeling pipeline for SVS data, and report SOTA on Opencpop / ACE-KiSing / OpenSinger / PopCS.

Reproducibility: ✅ **Code + weights + data pipeline released.** GitHub: . Model card + checkpoint: (Apache 2.0). ~400 new AST vocabulary tokens, `uv` install instructions included.

Paper #4 · Xu, 2026

Libretto — Symbolic Music for LLM Agents

arXiv:2606.22708 · Submitted 21 Jun 2026

arXiv: arXiv:2606.22708

Summary: Libretto is not an AMT paper in the strict sense, but it's the missing peer to MuseAgent-1: it tackles the *output* side. It gives an LLM agent a text-native symbolic-music grammar and a statistical "music cloud" so the agent can retrieve, diagnose, and *self-revise* generated MIDI. Important for the AMT↔agent loop because it makes the agent's edits auditable on musical-structure axes, not vibes.

Reproducibility: ⚠️ **Paper only.** No code link in the abstract. Worth a follow-up message to the author (Yichen Xu, UC Berkeley) for a repo.

Paper #5 · Bhandari et al., 2026

Text2Score — Sheet-Music Generation with an LLM Planner

arXiv:2605.13431 · Submitted 13 May 2026 (v2: 29 Jul 2026)

arXiv: arXiv:2605.13431

Summary: Text2Score is the inverse of AMT: text → score. The reason it earns a slot here is the *architecture* — an LLM plans musical structure bar-by-bar (key, time signature, instruments, harmony) and a smaller generator executes the notes, decoupling musical reasoning from token-level synthesis. Beats both a pure LLM agent and three end-to-end baselines on expert evaluation. Useful conceptual sibling to MuseAgent and Libretto.

Reproducibility: ✅ **Code, dataset, eval set, and prompts all open.** GitHub: . Demo: . CC BY 4.0.

Paper #6 · Yang et al., 2026

HeartMuLa — Open-Source Music Foundation Model Family

arXiv:2601.10547 · Submitted 15 Jan 2026 (v3: 7 Jul 2026)

arXiv: arXiv:2601.10547

Summary: HeartMuLa is a Chinese-academic attempt to reproduce a Suno-class song-generation system end-to-end. The reason it matters for AMT-watchers is **HeartTranscriptor** — a dedicated lyric-recognition model that operates as the *vocal-side* counterpart to a Melodia/MT3-style note transcriber. It also bundles a 12.5 Hz music codec, which is the right frame-rate regime for long-form symbolic reasoning. Suno-level quality on academic-scale data is the headline claim.

Reproducibility: ✅ **Open-source release.** Repo + model zoo via the first author's GitHub (Dongchao Yang / Zhejiang University + ByteDance). Apache-style code license; weights gated. Worth flagging because the bundle is the most ambitious "music LLM" stack from a non-US group to date.

Paper #7 · Chaturvedi et al., 2026

Advancing Multi-Instrument Music Transcription — 2025 AMT Challenge Results

arXiv:2603.27528 · Submitted 29 Mar 2026

arXiv: arXiv:2603.27528

Summary: The community benchmark for 2025/2026. Top systems (MIROS at F1 ≈0.60, YourMT3-YPTF-MoE-M at ≈0.59) both use YourMT3-style encoder–decoder Transformers with mixture-of-experts, not LLMs. The big takeaway is that *purely* token-prediction transformer architectures still beat the LALM/orchestration-style models on the raw metric — but only on a fixed eval; the LALM approach wins on flexibility and OOD.

Reproducibility: ⚠️ **Challenge artifacts public, per-team code varies.** Leaderboard and eval protocol at the 2025 AMT Challenge site. Several top teams (MIROS, YourMT3 variants) have GitHub repos; check the appendix of the paper.

Paper #8 · Jiang et al., 2025

MuFun — Unified Foundation Model for Music Understanding

arXiv:2508.01178 · Submitted 2 Aug 2025

arXiv: arXiv:2508.01178

Summary: MuFun is an end-to-end trainable foundation model that consumes interleaved audio+text, with score transcription as a *first-class* training objective alongside genre/tagging/QA. Long-context 390 s training window. Less flashy than MuScriptor but conceptually important: it's an argument that the right unit isn't "one model per MIR task" but a single shared backbone.

Reproducibility: ⚠️ **Paper open, code not in abstract.** The authors (Yi Jiang et al., ByteDance) have a MuCUE benchmark page; no public checkpoint or repo link in v1 as of this writing.

Paper #9 · Penarrubia et al., 2026

MuSViT — First Foundation Vision Model for Sheet Music

arXiv:2606.31811 · Submitted 30 Jun 2026 (accepted at ECCV '26)

arXiv: arXiv:2606.31811

Summary: The sheet-music-side counterpart to MuScriptor. Pre-trains a ViT on **9.7 M IMSLP pages** with a synthetic→real curriculum, then evaluates on full-page and staff-level OMR, symbol detection, and difficulty classification. Important complement to MuseAgent-1, which uses OMR as a perceptual front-end — MuSViT is the encoder that front-end *should* be using.

Reproducibility: ⚠️ **Not yet at v1.** Abstract doesn't link a repo; check the ECCV '26 camera-ready.

Paper #10 · Komiya & Fukuhara, 2024→2026

AMT-APC — Fine-Tuning an AMT Model for Piano Cover

arXiv:2409.14086 · Submitted 21 Sep 2024 (v2: 4 Jul 2026)

arXiv: arXiv:2409.14086

Summary: A 2024 paper that resurfaced this week (v2 dropped 4 Jul 2026). It fine-tunes hFT-Transformer (a piano-AMT model) as the backbone for automatic piano cover generation — i.e. it uses an *AMT* model as a *generation* prior. Small but conceptually clean evidence that AMT models carry enough musical structure to seed downstream symbolic-music tasks.

Reproducibility: ⚠️ **Paper open, no repo link in abstract.** Authors are at Hosei University, JP.
