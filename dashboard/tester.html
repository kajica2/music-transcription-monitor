Solo Instrument Transcription Tester

Browser-only · Web Audio + ACF2+

How this works. Pick or upload a solo instrument audio file. The browser runs monophonic pitch detection (autocorrelation) on the audio buffer, groups consecutive same-pitch frames into note events, and renders a piano roll. The detected notes then feed a multi-instrument description (key, range, suggested arrangement) — useful for stress-testing what a real transcription pipeline would see. Best on solo, clean recordings (≤30s). Polyphonic audio will give noisy results.
